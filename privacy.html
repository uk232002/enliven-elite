2. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Consultation requests — your name, phone number and the location and scope of your project, plus anything else you choose to write in the form.
  • Messages to the AI design assistant — the questions you type or speak and any photographs you attach (see section 4).
  • Direct contact — whatever you share when you call, email or message us on WhatsApp.

Information collected automatically

  • Server logs — like every website, our web server records your IP address, browser type, the pages and files requested, the date and time and the referring page. We use these to keep the site running and secure.
  • Usage analytics — only if you accept analytics cookies: which pages you view, how long you stay, your device and browser type, and your approximate location (city level), collected through Google Analytics.
  • Preferences stored in your browser — your colour theme and your cookie choice (see section 5).

We do not ask for, and ask you not to send us, sensitive information such as financial account details, government ID numbers or health information through the website.

4. The AI design assistant

The chat assistant on our home page answers general questions about interiors, architecture and construction. When you use it:

  • Your messages and any photographs are sent to our server and passed to Google’s Gemini API to generate a reply. Google processes them under its own terms and privacy policy, and its servers may be located outside India.
  • We keep the conversation only in the server’s memory, for at most six hours, so the assistant can follow the thread. We do not write your messages or photographs to disk.
  • We count requests per IP address each day to enforce fair-use limits.
  • If you use the microphone, speech is converted to text by your browser. Some browsers (for example Google Chrome) send the audio to their own speech service to do this.

Please do not share personal information about yourself or others, or photographs showing people, in the assistant. Its answers are general guidance, not professional advice — see our Terms & Conditions.

6. Who we share it with

We share personal data only with service providers who help us run the website and the studio, and only as far as they need it:

  • our web hosting provider, which stores the website and the messages sent through it;
  • our email provider, which delivers consultation requests to our inbox;
  • Google, for the AI assistant (Gemini API) and, if you consent, for analytics (Google Analytics);
  • WhatsApp (Meta), Google Maps and your phone or email service, but only when you choose to contact us or find us through them;
  • government authorities or courts, where the law requires us to disclose information.

Some of these providers process data outside India. Where they do, we rely on the protections in their terms and on the DPDP Act’s rules for transfers.

7. How long we keep it

  • Consultation requests — up to 24 months after our last contact with you. If you become a client, we keep project records for as long as the law requires, usually eight years for tax and accounting records.
  • AI assistant conversations — at most six hours, in server memory only.
  • Server logs — up to 90 days, unless we need them longer to investigate abuse.
  • Analytics data — up to 14 months in Google Analytics.

When data is no longer needed we delete it, or anonymise it so it no longer identifies you.

8. How we protect it

The website is served only over encrypted HTTPS connections. Access to consultation requests is limited to the people at the studio who need it. Our servers apply rate limits and spam filtering to the forms. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data, and we will notify you and the Data Protection Board of India of a personal data breach as the law requires.

9. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you and how we are using it;
  • correct, complete or update it;
  • erase it, where we no longer need it for the purpose you gave it for or to meet a legal obligation;
  • withdraw your consent at any time — this does not affect anything we did before you withdrew it;
  • nominate another person to exercise these rights if you die or become unable to act.

To use any of these rights, email info@enlivenelite.in. We will respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

10. Children

This website is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “last updated” date at the top shows when it last changed. If a change significantly affects how we use your data, we will say so clearly on the website.
